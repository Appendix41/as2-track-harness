The banner and the social card were generated locally (PIL) and contain no
third-party artwork. The three screenshots were captured by the authors on their
own test bench; the RGB frames show their lab, and no people are identifiable in
them. Re-check them if you publish a fork with your own hardware.

The dashboard UI is currently Chinese-labelled (see the roadmap); the JSON API,
the logs and every document in this repository are in English.
